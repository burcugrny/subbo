Subbo: subscription tracker for iPhone

Don't pay a subscription to track subscriptions.

Subbo shows what you really pay every month across all your subscriptions, in any currency. It reminds you before renewals and before free trials turn into paid plans, and helps you split shared plans with family and friends.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone

  • Multi-currency totals at today's exchange rates
  • Free-trial alerts and one-tap cancel links
  • Shared subscriptions and who owes you what
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets, iCloud sync (Pro), CSV export
  • No account, no bank login, no ads, no tracking

Questions

Is Subbo free?

Yes. You can track up to 3 subscriptions for free. Subbo Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited subscriptions, widgets and iCloud sync. There is no subscription.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. You add subscriptions yourself in seconds. Subbo never asks for your bank login and does not collect your data.

How do I stop a free trial from charging me?

Add the trial with its end date. Subbo sends a reminder before the trial ends, with a link to the cancel page.
