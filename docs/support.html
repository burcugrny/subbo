Support

Need help or have an idea? Email subbosupport@gmail.com. We usually answer within two business days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Subbo requires iOS 17 or later.

Is Subbo free?

You can track up to 3 subscriptions for free. Subbo Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited subscriptions, more reminder options, widgets and iCloud sync. It is not a subscription.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open Subbo, go to Settings and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

iCloud sync isn't working

iCloud sync is part of Pro and starts the next time you open the app after buying. Check that iCloud is turned on in the Settings app, under your name > iCloud.

A total looks wrong

Subscriptions in other currencies are converted at the day's exchange rate, so totals change slightly over time. Free trials are not counted until they end. For shared subscriptions, only your share is counted.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications for Subbo are allowed in the Settings app, and that the subscription has a reminder set.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your data moves with an iPhone backup or Quick Start. With Pro, it also syncs through iCloud. You can always export a CSV file from Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete subscriptions in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on the device. To delete Subbo's iCloud data, see our Privacy Policy.
