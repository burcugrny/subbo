Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Subbo is a subscription tracker built to work without collecting your data. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter and to the few requests the app makes.

Who we are

Subbo is developed by Burcu Girenay Gökduman (Türkiye), who is responsible for this app. Contact: subbosupport@gmail.com.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • We, the developer, never receive the subscriptions, prices, names, notes or any other data you enter.

What we collect

The app sends us nothing. The only information we ever receive is what you send us yourself by email, for example a support request. We use your email address and message only to answer you, do not share them, and delete them within 12 months.

Where your data is stored

Your subscriptions are stored on your device. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a short summary that also stays on your device. If you have Subbo Pro and iCloud is turned on, your subscriptions are also synced through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy; we cannot see or access it.

Exchange rates

To convert currencies, Subbo downloads public exchange rates about once a day from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request contains no personal information and none of your subscription data. As with any internet request, the provider can see your device's IP address.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. They are not sent through any server.

Purchases

Subbo Pro is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Export: Settings > Export as CSV.
  • Delete one item: delete the subscription in the app. With iCloud sync it is also deleted from iCloud.
  • Delete everything on this device: delete the app.
  • Delete Subbo's iCloud data: in the iPhone Settings app, tap your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Subbo > Delete Data.
  • Support emails: write to us and we will delete them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or KVKK in Türkiye), you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we do not hold any of your app data, these rights mainly concern support emails. Contact us at subbosupport@gmail.com.

Children

Subbo is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
